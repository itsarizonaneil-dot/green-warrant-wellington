What We Do

A booking service, not a Certification Authority

Green Warrant Wellington exists to make one thing simple: getting a campervan or motorhome self-containment certified in the Wellington region. We handle the enquiry and booking side, and connect you with a licensed PGDB-authorised Certification Authority who carries out the actual inspection and issues your green warrant.

We're not a Certification Authority ourselves, and we don't inspect vehicles or issue certificates. Being upfront about that matters to us — you should always know exactly who is certifying your vehicle.

Our certifying partner

Certifying partner name, PGDB registration/CA number, and contact details will be added here once confirmed. Until then, all enquiries are handled directly and matched with an appropriate licensed Certification Authority.
