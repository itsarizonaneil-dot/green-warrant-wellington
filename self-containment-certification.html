What It Is

Why green warrant certification matters

Self-containment certification (the "green warrant") confirms your vehicle can hold fresh water, grey water and waste for freedom camping without needing external facilities. It's issued under the Self-Contained Vehicles Regulations 2023 by a PGDB-authorised Certification Authority — not by any council or by us directly.

Since 7 June 2026, blue warrants have no longer been valid anywhere in New Zealand. Every vehicle used for freedom camping needs a current green warrant, and freedom camping without valid certification can result in a $400 fine.

Certification is valid for 4 years from the date of issue, after which your vehicle needs a revalidation inspection — see our renewal page for that process.

What's inspected

  • A permanently fixed toilet — portable toilets no longer qualify a vehicle as self-contained
  • Fresh water tank capacity and fittings
  • Grey water and waste water storage
  • Sealed waste systems and correct outlets

Exact requirements are confirmed at inspection by the Certification Authority — this list is a general guide, not a substitute for the official checklist.

Cost

What certification costs

Typical certification cost is NZ$183–280, which includes the NZ$120 PGDB levy. The exact fee depends on the Certification Authority and any retrofit work your vehicle needs beforehand.

If your vehicle doesn't yet have a fixed toilet or adequate tanks, retrofit and plumbing work is priced separately — see our retrofit & plumbing page.
