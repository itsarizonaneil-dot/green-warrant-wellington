Why This Matters

The fixed-toilet requirement catches a lot of owners out

A key change under the Self-Contained Vehicles Regulations 2023 is that a permanently fixed toilet is now required — a portable toilet no longer qualifies a vehicle as self-contained, no matter how it was certified previously.

If your vehicle doesn't yet meet the fixed-toilet or fresh/grey/black water tank requirements, that's retrofit and plumbing work, not something a Certification Authority inspection alone can fix. We connect you with the certifying partner (or a partnered plumber) who can quote the retrofit before booking your certification inspection.

Typical retrofit work

  • Fitting a permanently fixed toilet
  • Installing or upsizing fresh water tanks
  • Installing grey and black water storage
  • Sealed waste plumbing and correct outlet fittings

Retrofit work is quoted separately from certification, based on your vehicle and current setup.
