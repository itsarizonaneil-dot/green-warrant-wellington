The 4-Year Cycle

Why renewal matters

A green warrant is valid for 4 years from its issue date. Once it lapses, your vehicle is no longer certified for freedom camping — even if nothing about it has changed. Freedom camping without valid certification can result in a $400 fine, and there's no fallback to an old blue warrant — those stopped being valid on 7 June 2026.

Renewal is a fresh inspection by a PGDB-authorised Certification Authority, checking your fixed toilet and water/waste systems still meet the current requirements under the Self-Contained Vehicles Regulations 2023. It's usually simpler than first-time certification if nothing on the vehicle has changed.

Renewal at a glance

  • Book ahead of your 4-year expiry — don't wait until it lapses
  • Same fixed-toilet and tank checks as first-time certification
  • Typically NZ$183–280, same as new certification
  • Avoids re-buying or re-fitting a compliant setup from scratch

Don't let your certification lapse

An expired green warrant means no valid self-containment status, regardless of how your vehicle is fitted out. If you're not sure when your current certification expires, check the date on your certificate or get in touch and we'll help you work it out.
