How It Works

Three steps to a valid green warrant

1

Book

Call or send an enquiry with your vehicle details and suburb. We match you with an available Certification Authority inspection slot nearby.

2

Inspection

The Certification Authority checks your fixed toilet, water tanks and waste systems against the Self-Contained Vehicles Regulations 2023.

3

Certified

Pass, and you're issued a valid green warrant — good for 4 years of freedom camping across New Zealand.

Blue warrants stop being valid nationwide from 7 June 2026

From that date, every vehicle used for freedom camping must hold a valid green warrant under the Self-Contained Vehicles Regulations 2023. Freedom camping without valid certification can result in a $400 fine. A permanently fixed toilet is now required — portable toilets no longer qualify a vehicle as self-contained.
